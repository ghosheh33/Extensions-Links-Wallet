1. Introduction
Mahmoud Ghosheh ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the "Link Manager" Chrome Extension collects, uses, and protects your information.

2. Information We Collect
When you use the Link Manager extension, we only access and collect the following data when you explicitly click the "Save" button:

Page Title: The title of the active webpage.

Page URL: The web address of the active webpage.

User Input: The category names you manually type to organize your links.

3. How We Use Your Information
The collected data is used strictly for the core functionality of the extension:

To save and display your bookmarked links.

To organize your links into the categories you created.

To allow you to search and manage your saved links.

4. Data Storage and Security
All data collected by the Link Manager extension is stored locally on your device using the chrome.storage.local API.

We do not transmit your data to any external servers.

We do not have access to your saved links.

Your data remains entirely on your machine and is deleted if you uninstall the extension or manually delete the links.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. Your data is 100% private and accessible only by you.

6. User Rights
You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete any saved link directly from the extension's interface at any time.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
